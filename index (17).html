Trackers & Management: common questions

Can several people use the tracker at the same time?

Yes. Saved to OneDrive, SharePoint or Teams, Excel lets multiple people edit at once. Tell us if that’s your plan and we’ll design for it.

How many records can it handle?

Excel handles thousands of rows comfortably. If you’re working with far more, we’ll tell you and suggest the right approach.

Can it send reminders?

Excel can’t send emails on its own without extra tools, but we can make due and overdue items stand out automatically so nothing gets missed.

Can I add columns or categories later?

Yes. We build trackers so you can add categories and options without touching the formulas.
