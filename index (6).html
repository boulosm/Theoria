Budgets & Finance: common questions

Are you accountants?

No. We build the Excel tools; we don’t give accounting, tax or investment advice. Your spreadsheet can be set up to match how your accountant wants to see the numbers.

Can you build it from my existing spreadsheet?

Yes. Send your current file and we’ll keep what works and rebuild what doesn’t.

Can it compare different scenarios?

Yes. We can add inputs so you can change assumptions and instantly see the effect on cash flow or profit.

Can I use it for my personal or household budget?

Absolutely. Personal budgets are a great fit for the Starter package.
