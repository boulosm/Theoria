Automation & Power Query: common questions

What’s the difference between Power Query and macros?

Power Query is built into Excel and is ideal for importing, cleaning and combining data. It’s safe, needs no special settings and is easy to maintain. Macros (VBA) can do more, but need to be enabled and are harder to edit. We use whichever fits the job.

Do I need to know Power Query to use it?

No. Once it’s set up, you just click Refresh. We’ll include instructions.

Will it still work next month?

Yes, as long as your source files keep the same general layout. If your software changes its export format, small adjustments may be needed.

How much time will it save?

It depends on the task, but work that takes an hour or more each week often drops to a few minutes.
