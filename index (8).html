Built around how you work

Spreadsheets tend to grow over time: a new tab here, a pasted formula there. Eventually something breaks, or nobody is sure which numbers to trust. We figure out what’s going on and fix it, without starting over unless that really is the better option.

What we can build

Broken formulas

#REF!, #VALUE!, #N/A and totals that just don’t add up.

Lookup problems

VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP and INDEX/MATCH that return the wrong result.

Slow workbooks

Files that take ages to open, calculate or save.

Messy layout

Inconsistent formatting, hidden rows and confusing tabs.

Inherited spreadsheets

Excel Fix & Clean-up: common questions

How do I send you my file?

Attach it to your quote request email. If it contains sensitive information, you can send a copy with names or numbers replaced by sample data.

What if it can’t be fixed?

We’ll look at the file before quoting. If a rebuild makes more sense than a repair, we’ll explain why and quote that instead, and you decide.

Will you change how my spreadsheet works?

Only what you ask for. We’ll keep your layout and process unless you want improvements.

Can you tell me why it broke?

Yes. We’ll include a short note on what caused the problem and how to avoid it.
