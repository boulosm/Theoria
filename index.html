About Theoria Analytics

We’re a Toronto-based team of Excel geeks who have spent more than 10 years building spreadsheets, dashboards and data tools, and we still love it.

Excel geeks, for over 10 years

We’ve been working in Excel for more than a decade, and we’re genuinely passionate about data. We enjoy the moment when a messy pile of numbers turns into something clear, like a total that finally reconciles, a dashboard that answers the question at a glance, or a report that used to take an afternoon and now takes one click.

Over those years we’ve seen how much time people lose to copy-pasting, re-doing calculations and wrestling with spreadsheets that grew messier every month. Most people only use a small part of what Excel can do. Theoria Analytics exists to close that gap.

Why we do this

Good data makes decisions easier. You bring the problem in your own words, and we design and build a workbook that turns your information into something you can actually use.

How we build

  • Practical over flashy. Every sheet, formula and chart has a job to do.
  • Easy to maintain. Clear inputs, protected formulas and an instructions tab, so the file keeps working after we hand it over.
  • Built for your version. We design around the Excel you actually have.
  • Upfront and honest. Fixed quotes, and we’ll tell you if something isn’t a good fit for Excel.

Based in Toronto, working everywhere

We’re based in Toronto, Ontario. Everything is handled by email, so we work with clients across the GTA, the rest of Canada and beyond.

Who we work with

Small businesses, contractors and trades, non-profits, teams inside larger companies, event organizers and individuals with a personal project. If it can be tracked, calculated, organized or automated in Excel, it’s in scope.
