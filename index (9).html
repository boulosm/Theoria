Calculators & Tools: common questions

Can I stop staff from breaking the formulas?

Yes. We protect the formula cells and leave only the input cells editable.

Can it produce a printable quote for my customers?

Yes. We can add a branded quote sheet that fills itself in from the calculator.

What happens when my prices change?

Prices and rates live in a settings tab. Update them there and every calculation updates.

Can it work on a phone or tablet?

Simple calculators can work in the Excel mobile app. Let us know if that matters and we’ll design for it.
