Built around how you work

Most businesses already have the data. It’s just scattered across exports, tabs and copy-pasted tables. A good dashboard pulls it together so you can answer questions in seconds instead of rebuilding the same report every month.

What we can build

Sales dashboard

Revenue by month, product, customer, region or sales rep.

KPI scorecard

The handful of numbers you check every week, with targets and trends.

Monthly financial report

Income, expenses and profit compared against budget.

Project dashboard

Status, budget and progress across every active job or project.

Marketing report

Leads, spend and results by channel or campaign.

Operations dashboard

Jobs completed, turnaround times, capacity and workload.

Don’t see your exact use? These are just common requests. Describe yours and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

What’s included

  • A clean data sheet that the dashboard reads from, so updating it is just adding rows
  • Charts and KPI tiles chosen for what you actually need to see
  • Filters and slicers to drill into a month, client, product or person
  • PivotTables where they make the report easier to maintain
  • A print- and PDF-friendly layout for sharing
  • A short instructions tab so anyone on your team can use it

Who it’s for

Owners, managers and teams who spend hours building the same report by hand, or who have plenty of data but no quick way to see what it means.

Dashboards & Reports: common questions

Will my dashboard update automatically?

Yes. When you add new data to the input sheet, the charts and totals update. If your data comes from exports, we can set up Power Query so a single Refresh click pulls in the latest file.

Can it use data from my accounting software, CRM or POS?

Usually, yes. If your system can export to Excel or CSV, we can build the dashboard around that export.

Which version of Excel do I need?

Tell us which version you use when you ask for a quote. We build around it. Some newer features need Microsoft 365, and if you don’t have it we’ll use approaches that work on your version.

Can you improve a dashboard I already have?

Yes. Send it over and we’ll quote on cleaning it up, fixing what’s broken or adding what’s missing.
