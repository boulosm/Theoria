Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with us. Can’t find your answer? Just ask.

Getting started

Do I need to know Excel to work with you?

No. Just explain what you’re trying to track, calculate, organize or automate in your own words. Choosing the formulas and features is our job.

How much does a custom Excel spreadsheet cost?

Packages start at $99 for fixes, $149 for a focused custom workbook, $349 for advanced builds and $699 for complete business systems. You’ll get a fixed quote before any work begins.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Describe what you need and we’ll reply with the scope and price. There’s no commitment until you say go.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the size of the project. Small fixes are quick; larger systems take longer. You’ll get a timeline with your quote.

Your workbook

Do I own the finished file?

Yes. Your completed Excel workbook is yours to keep, use and build on.

Can you work with a spreadsheet I already have?

Yes. You can send your existing file with your request. We can fix it, improve it or use it as the starting point for something new.

Which versions of Excel do you support?

Tell us which version you use (Microsoft 365, a standalone version, Windows or Mac) and we’ll build around it.

Can you build it in Google Sheets instead?

We specialize in Excel. Many simple workbooks also work in Google Sheets, but not every feature does. Mention it in your request and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

Do you use macros?

Only when they’re the best tool for the job. Most builds use formulas and Power Query, which need no special settings.

Support & privacy

What if I have questions after delivery?

Reach out any time. You’ll get help from a real person, not a ticket queue.

Is my data kept private?

Your files are used only to build and support your workbook. If your data is sensitive, you can send a copy with sample data instead. See our privacy policy for details.

Where are you located?

We’re based in Toronto, Ontario. Everything is handled by email, so we work with clients across the GTA, Canada and beyond.

Still have a question?

Send us a quick message. You’ll get a reply from a real person.
