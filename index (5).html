Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools. We only receive information you choose to send us by email: your name, email address, the details of your project and any files you attach.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare a quote, build and deliver your workbook, and provide support afterwards. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Your files

Files you send are used only to work on your project. If your data is sensitive, you’re welcome to send a copy with names, figures or other details replaced by sample data. You can ask us to delete your files and messages at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing theoriaanalytics@gmail.com.

Changes

If we add analytics or other tools to this site, we’ll update this page.
