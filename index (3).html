How it works

You explain it. We Excel it. Here’s what happens from your first message to a finished workbook.

Step 1

Tell us what you need.

Explain the problem, your current process or the spreadsheet you wish existed. Plain language is perfect.

Step 2

Get a free quote.

We confirm what we’ll build, what it costs and when it’ll be ready, before any work starts.

Step 3

We build it around you.

Your workbook is designed for your workflow, not squeezed into a generic template.

Step 4

Use it, with support.

Get a clean, practical file you own. Questions afterwards? Ask a real person.

Not sure what you need? That’s okay.

You don’t need to know whether you need formulas, a dashboard, automation, Power Query or PivotTables. Answer three simple questions and we’ll take it from there:

  • What are you doing now? How do you currently track, calculate, organize or report the information?
  • What’s taking too long? The repetitive work, confusing spreadsheet or manual process you want to improve.
  • What would be better? Describe the outcome in plain language. We’ll figure out the Excel part.

What to include in your request

  • A short description of what you want the workbook to do
  • Your current spreadsheet, if you have one (a copy with sample data is fine)
  • Any exports or reports the workbook should use
  • Which version of Excel you use, and whether others will share the file
  • Your deadline, if you have one

Don’t have all of that? Send what you have. We’ll ask if we need more.
